Quick brewing guide

French press

For a French press, use coarsely ground coffee and add hot water. Allow the coffee to steep for around four minutes before gently pressing the plunger down. Pour the coffee into your cup and serve immediately.

Pour-over

For pour-over coffee, use a medium-fine grind and rinse the filter with hot water before brewing. Add the coffee grounds and slowly pour hot water over them in circular movements, allowing the coffee to bloom before continuing the pour.

Espresso

For espresso, use finely ground coffee and distribute the grounds evenly in the portafilter. Tamp the coffee consistently before placing the portafilter into the machine and starting the extraction. After brewing, clean the group head to keep the espresso machine ready for the next use.
